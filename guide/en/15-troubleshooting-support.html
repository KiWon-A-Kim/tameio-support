15. Troubleshooting and Support

Check these first

  • The balance differs from my bank app → check for missing or duplicated transactions, then use the balance adjustment in chapter 3.
  • The budget figures look wrong → check that the identity from chapter 6 holds (available pool + amount left in every budget group). If your payday and the start of the budget period differ, the pool can temporarily look short.
  • I pasted a message and nothing was filled in → follow chapter 8 to create a new message recognition rule.
  • A recurring rule's scheduled transactions look odd → if you have just changed the rule's date, see step 4 in chapter 7. Clearing the earlier scheduled item and keeping only the new date is the correct behavior.

What to prepare before contacting support

When you write in through the Support link in Settings, having the following ready will get you help much faster.

  • Which screen you were on, and the order in which you did things.
  • What you expected, and what you actually saw.
  • For a message recognition problem, an example that reproduces only the shape of the message (where the line breaks are, which word sits where) instead of real amounts, account numbers, and names — see the privacy notes in chapter 8.

Sending the information above by support email or through the in-app link makes it far quicker to reproduce and fix.

The app has no feature that bundles diagnostic logs or version information into one report. The Support item in Settings is only a link that opens a fixed support web page, so you will need to type the version (see Settings → App version) and the reproduction details yourself when you get in touch.
